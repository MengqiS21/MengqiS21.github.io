Accessibility and Inclusive Interaction

I investigate how LLM agents and interactive systems can identify and reduce accessibility barriers. My work spans web and mobile accessibility auditing and repair, alongside accessible educational play for children with motor impairments.

I contribute to system development, WCAG 2.2-based benchmarks, and evaluation, as well as collaborative physical and interactive prototyping. Across these projects, I aim to support participation for people whose needs are often overlooked in mainstream interface design.

"Accessible systems should reduce barriers to participation and give people more ways to interact on their own terms."

Research Questions

  • How can LLM agents gather and use interaction evidence to evaluate web accessibility?
  • How can benchmarks grounded in WCAG 2.2 support the development and evaluation of accessibility auditing systems?
  • How can physical and interactive prototypes reduce input demands and support educational play for children with motor impairments?

Selected Project Areas

  • LLM- and agent-driven approaches to detecting, assessing, and repairing accessibility issues in web and mobile interfaces.
  • A WCAG 2.2-based web accessibility benchmark, including mock-up pages for system evaluation.
  • PlayBridge: collaborative design and iterative prototyping of accessible educational games for children with diverse motor abilities.
